# Asset sources and ownership

This inventory records what is supported by the supplied documents and retained project files. All approved website assets remain byte-identical during this migration. The lab supplied and approved the material for this website, but that alone does not establish ownership of every embedded figure or photograph. Unspecified licenses, permissions, and mandatory credits remain **unverified**; no third-party ownership is claimed.

Original supplied files, embedded media, source dimensions, and SHA-256 hashes are retained in the separate private backup. `audit/asset-provenance.json` maps published derivatives to their source media. No original image is enlarged to suggest unavailable resolution. No replacement figure or image has been generated for this launch.
